August 23, 2007

Hellcab!

Hello fans! Carol started rehearsals this week for her upcoming show, The Crucible. Not satisfied with just one show, Carol has been continuing to audition for other area theaters, and has just been cast in a production of Hellcab, with Bolingbrook's Theatre-on-the-Hill!

The Crucible runs from October 4 - 14 in DeKalb, and Hellcab will run October 26 - November 18 in Bolingbrook (click show titles above for detailed show dates, times, and tickets). It's going to be a busy month of rehearsals!

Father Tony Zamboni's Christmas Shorts

Merry Christmas, fans!

Yes, I know it's only just past Thanksgiving. But what better way to prepare for the Christmas season by seeing our favorite thespian in a Christmas-themed show? You can do just that by visiting the James Downing Theatre this coming weekend.

What is it? Father Tony Zamboni's Christmas Shorts... a holiday-themed short play festival.

When is it? I just said... it's this weekend, December 5th and 6th at 7:30 PM, and 7th at 1:30 PM.

Where is it? The James Downing Theatre 6740 N. Oliphant Ave. Chicago, IL 60631

**

Tickets are $10. To reserve a seat, call (224) 725-3696, or click here.

April 9, 2016

Plaza Suite

We're back! I realize it has been over a year since our last posting. I guess that's what happens when life gets in the way. Carol has been keeping busy this past year with many of her odd jobs, but less stage acting... until now!

Later this month, Carol returns to the familiar Gallery Theater stage with their production of Neil Simon's Plaza Suite, but this time with the double duty of both acting and directing! Carol will be directing one act of this three act play, and appearing on stage in another.

"When and where can I witness this theatrical thunderbolt of talent; this dynamic dramatic directorial debut; this amazingly artistic emotional expression?!!", I hear you cry.

Show dates: APRIL 22, 23, 24, 29, 30, MAY 1 Friday & Saturday shows are at 7:30 pm, Sunday shows at 3:00 pm

Gallery Theater 203 Turner Ct. West Chicago, IL

Tickets are $14 Regular Admission - $12 for Seniors & Students

Gallery Theater is a small intimate venue, so shows are often sold out. Reservations are encouraged.

For ticket reservations or information click here, or email gallerytickets@aol.com or call (630) 234-5919.

Rory's First Kiss Casting Notice

Hello RFK/Bat Fans! Rumor has it that the extra casting company for Rory's First Kiss has changed. The new casting company can be found at http://rfkcasting.com. They are accepting applications for extras, either by mail or electronically (details and application form are on the site). Good luck!

7/19 UPDATE: They are apparently not accepting online submissions (and have removed the text from the web site that said that they were). Snail Mail submissions only.

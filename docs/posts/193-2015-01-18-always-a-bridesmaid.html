Always A Bridesmaid

Greetings fans, and welcome to 2015! I trust your new year is off to a great start; but if you need a little injection of entertainment and comedy, mark your calendars to see Carol's upcoming show, Always a Bridesmaid.

This is the LOL comedic tale of four high school BFFs who make a promise on the night of their Senior Prom to be in each other's weddings. Three decades later, these friends-for-life are still determined to honor that vow as they navigate the choppy waters of love and matrimony, committed to the notion that careers, waistlines and even marriages may disappear, but real friendships last a lifetime. Carol plays Monette, a kinda flashy, kinda trashy, southern-to-the-bone flirt. You won't want to miss it!

Where: Gallery Theater 203 Turner Ct., West Chicago, IL 60185

When: February 27, 28, March 1, 7, 8. Friday & Saturdays 7:30 PM, Sundays 3:00 PM

Cost: $12

For Reservations & Information, contact Gallery Theater at 630-234-5919 or gallerytickets@aol.com

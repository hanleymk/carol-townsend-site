April 1, 2013

Twisted Olive and Friends

Happy New Year!

Better late than never. That statement applies to both my new year sentiments, and the notification of Carol's return to the stage... starting this weekend!! (and no... despite the date today, this is not a joke!)

Evergreen Theatre Ensemble presents Twisted Olive and Friends, an evening of short plays. The show will be a presentation of eight short plays. Our own Carol will be appearing as Mrs. Olive Sorken, in the eponymous Mrs. Sorken; Angie in So Tell Me About This Guy; and Giovanna in Sack of Grievances.

Performed at:

Center Stage Theater

1665 Quincy Ave. #13

Naperville, IL

Show Dates are:

Friday, April 5 & 12 at 8:00 PM

Saturday, April 6 & 13 at 8:00 PM

Sunday, April 7 at 3:00 PM

Tickets are $20 for adults, $15 for students and seniors.

Contact the box office at 630-355-9212.

If you're a denizen of The Facebook, you can find an event for the show here.

See you there!

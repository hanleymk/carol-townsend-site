Love, Loss, and What I Wore

Greetings fans!

Spring is upon us, and it is the perfect time to see a show! Why not come to see Carol in Love, Loss, and What I Wore? Ladies, bring your men!

Performances are at 8:00pm on May 2nd, 3rd, 9th, and 10th, and 3:00pm on May the 4th (come out to celebrate Star Wars Day!)

Location is Center Stage Theatre in Naperville, 1665 Quincy Ave.,

Tickets are $20 for Adults, $15 for Students/Seniors, or $10 for children under 12 (but you probably don't want to bring your children under 12... just sayin'). For tickets and more information, click here.

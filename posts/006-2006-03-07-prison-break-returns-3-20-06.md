# Prison Break Returns 3/20/06

*Originally published 2006-03-07 on the Blogspot site*

There have been no new episodes of Prison Break broadcast since 11/28/05, but in that time they have been filming the remaining season's episodes in the Chicago area.  Carol has been very busy shooting many scenes in the upcoming episodes, which will start airing on FOX on 3/20/06 (check your local listings!)  Be sure to watch for Carol in these key roles:

- A nurse in the prison infirmary, walking down the hall after Lincoln passes in shackles (might be on the cutting room floor)

- A pedestrian in a scene in the street with Veronica and Nick (episode 14, see post above, [Prison Break Update ...](http://caroltownsendfan.blogspot.com/2006/03/prison-break-update-episode-14.html))

- An attorney in the courtroom following the Pedestrian scene with Veronica and Nick (episode 14, see post above, [Prison Break Update ...](http://caroltownsendfan.blogspot.com/2006/03/prison-break-update-episode-14.html))

- A Cubs fan (with long brown hair) at Wrigley Field, in a flashback with young Lincoln and his father

- A reporter at a Vice Presidential press conference
- The prosecuting attorney in a flashback to Lincoln's trial
- A patron in a restaurant in a flashback with Michael and Veronica

- A secret service agent at the Secret Service Agency

- A commuter on the "L" platform

- A pedestrian in a diner scene, outside the diner window

- A reporter (with blonde hair!) at a Vice Presidential press conference (possibly the season finale?)

Keep an eye on this site for more details and hopefully screen shots as the episodes are aired!

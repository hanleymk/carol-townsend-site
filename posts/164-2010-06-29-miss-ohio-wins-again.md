# Miss Ohio Wins Again!

*Originally published 2010-06-29 on the Blogspot site*

Just a quick note... [Miss Ohio](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1286748/), in which Carol played Natalie's mother Janet, was screened at the [Visionfest 2010](http://www.visionfest.com/film_festival/awards/) film festival in New York City last week, and came away with two more awards:  **Best Picture** and **Best Director**.  Congratulations to Gregory Fitzsimmons (the best director), Carol and the entire cast and crew!

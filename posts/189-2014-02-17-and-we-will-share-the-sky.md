# And We Will Share The Sky

*Originally published 2014-02-17 on the Blogspot site*

> **Note:** this same post also appears on the other site — https://www.caroltownsend.com/blog/2014/2/17/and-we-will-share-the-sky. Both versions are kept; they differ slightly in length and each carries its own copy of the images.

![2014-02-17-and-we-will-share-the-sky__awwsts-poster-smaller.jpg](../images/2014-02-17-and-we-will-share-the-sky__awwsts-poster-smaller.jpg)

A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away...   No, wait-- that's a different story.  But no less epic!

Where did the sun come from?  Why is it dark at night?  Where does the moon get its glow?  Do giraffes poop in the bush?  These, and other questions of the ages will be answered in Carol's upcoming show.  Come and witness Carol extend her acting range as she disappears into the role of Gemma Giraffe!  You won't believe your eyes!

[EverGreen Theatre Ensemble](http://www.evergreentheatreensemble.org/) presents **And We Will Share The Sky**, by [Donna Latham](http://www.donnalatham.com/play-pages/and-we-will-share-the-sky.html).

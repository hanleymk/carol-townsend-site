# The Vision World Premiere

*Originally published 2009-03-15 on the Blogspot site*

It has been a busy week for Carol, with the screening of [Miss Ohio](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1286748/) earlier this week, and the World Premiere of [The Vision](http://thevisionmovie.com/index.htm) on Friday the 13th at the historic [Rialto Square Theatre](http://www.rialtosquare.com/) in Joliet, IL!    The Vision is an independent film by [Robert Alaniz](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm2792765/), shot entirely in the southwest Chicago suburbs.

*Originally published 2009-04-05 on the Blogspot site*

Marla Gusler (as Minnie Swisher) and Carol (as Sissy Brockman)

**Tales From Darkest Suburbia** at [Riverfront Playhouse](http://www.riverfrontplayhouse.com/) is half-way through its run.  If you have not seen the show yet, you only have until April 25th (but don't forget-- Carol will not be appearing on Aopril 17/18).  The show was mentioned in the online [Beacon News](http://www.suburbanchicagonews.com/beaconnews/entertainment/1496200,6_5_NA27_ARIVERFRONT_S1.article).  Carol appears above (fans will recognize the "Babs" wig, from Prison Break as shown [here](http://caroltownsendfan.blogspot.com/2006/05/dont-drink-water-spoilers.html)) as Sissy Brockman, in a tale entitled: "The Rubbing".

I will be at the show on April 24.  If you would like to join, just let me know!  You can reserve tickets by calling the theater at (630) 897-9496.

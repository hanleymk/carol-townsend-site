# Movin' Right Along...

*Originally published 2006-07-10 on the Blogspot site*

Carol's acting career is Movin' Right Along, as she has landed a part in an upcoming theater production of the same name.    [Soapbox Theatre Company](http://www.soapboxtheatre.com/) will be presenting Movin' Right Along (not to be confused with the [Muppet song](http://mhanley67.googlepages.com/Muppets_Movin.mp3) of the same name).   This show is a "one of a kind comedic variety show that is fun for everyone...  jam packed with pure entertainment through an original compilation of scenes, songs, dancing, comedy sketches, audience participation, and so much more!"

Details are still forthcoming, but rehearsals have begun, and Carol is still in shock and denial (yet very excited!) at having landed a part.  Stay tuned to the blog for showtimes and location.

Congratulations, Babes!

# Table and Chairs 2009

*Originally published 2009-10-12 on the Blogspot site*

Greetings Fans!

After a six month absence from the stage, Carol has been cast in the upcoming third annual **Table and Chairs Festival**, hosted by [n.u.f.a.n. ensemble](http://www.nufanensemble.com/).

**Table and Chairs** consists of ten one-act plays which use a table and chairs as the only set pieces.  Carol will appear in one of the ten plays entitled **Roles for Breakfast**.  The show is for two nights only: **Tuesday, October 20th** and **Wednesday, October 21st**, **8:00pm**.  at the **City Lit Theatre**, [1020 W. Bryn Mawr](http://maps.google.com/maps?f=q&source=s_q&hl=en&geocode=&q=1020+W+Bryn+Mawr+Avenue+Chicago,+Illinois+60660+&sll=37.0625,-95.677068&sspn=51.841773,89.560547&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=1020+W+Bryn+Mawr+Ave,+Chicago,+Cook,+Illinois+60640&t=h&z=16&iwloc=A) in Chicago.

Tickets are only $10-- that's only $1 per play!!  Where else can you receive so much entertainment for so little?  Tickets can be reserved [here](http://www.nufanensemble.com/nufan_ensemble/Shows/Entries/2009/10/20_Table_%26_Chairs_2009.html).  See you there!

# Hellcab!

*Originally published 2007-08-23 on the Blogspot site*

Hello fans!
Carol started rehearsals this week for her upcoming show, [The Crucible](http://www.stagecoachers.com/2007/2007_H.html).  Not satisfied with just one show, Carol has been continuing to audition for other area theaters, and has just been cast in a production of [Hellcab](http://tothbolingbrook.com/index_files/Page318.htm), with Bolingbrook's [Theatre-on-the-Hill](http://tothbolingbrook.com/index.htm)!

The Crucible runs from October 4 - 14 in DeKalb, and Hellcab will run October 26 - November 18  in Bolingbrook (click show titles above for detailed show dates, times, and tickets).  It's going to be a busy month of rehearsals!

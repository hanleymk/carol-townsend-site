# Crossing The Lake

*Originally published 2009-10-01 on the Blogspot site*

Carol adds another film to her dramatic oeuvre with an appearance in **Crossing the Lake**, an independent film by the [Caledonia Night Sky Co](http://www.caledonianightsky.com/crossingthelake/).  Carol plays Debbie, the sickly over-protective mother of the main character, Patrick.  Carol's scenes were filmed in Rockford this past weekend.

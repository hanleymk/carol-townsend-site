# Twisted Olive and Friends

*Originally published 2013-04-01 on the Blogspot site*

Happy New Year!

Better late than never.  That statement applies to both my new year sentiments, and the notification of Carol's return to the stage... starting this weekend!!  (and no... despite the date today, this is not a joke!)

[Evergreen Theatre Ensemble](http://evergreentheatreensemble.org/) presents **Twisted Olive and Friends**, an evening of short plays. The show will be a presentation of eight short plays. Our own Carol will be appearing as Mrs. Olive Sorken, in the eponymous *Mrs. Sorken*; Angie in *So Tell Me About This Guy*; and Giovanna in *Sack of Grievances*.

**Performed at**:

[Center Stage Theater](http://centerstage.fairladyproductions.net/)

1665 Quincy Ave. #13

Naperville, IL

**Show Dates are**:

Friday, April 5 & 12 at 8:00 PM

Saturday, April 6 & 13 at 8:00 PM

Sunday, April 7 at 3:00 PM

Tickets are $20 for adults, $15 for students and seniors.

Contact the box office at **630-355-9212**.

If you're a denizen of The Facebook, you can find an event for the show [here](https://www.facebook.com/events/406327446130024/).

See you there!

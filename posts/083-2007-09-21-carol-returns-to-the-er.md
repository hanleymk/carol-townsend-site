# Carol Returns to the ER

*Originally published 2007-09-21 on the Blogspot site*

Breaking news... tomorrow, Carol will be working on an [episode of ER](http://www.tv.com/er/gravity/episode/1127933/summary.html?tag=ep_list;ep_title;3) that will be filming at Wrigley Field.  The episode is scheduled to air on October 18th, according to [this article](http://chicagosports.chicagotribune.com/sports/baseball/cubs/cs-070907cubsbits,1,1393805.story?coll=cs-cubs-headlines), and will feature former Florida Marlin, Derek Lee (for you sports fans).  This will mark Carol's fourth appearance on ER (three prior appearances in season 13 can be found [here](http://caroltownsendfan.blogspot.com/2006/12/lady-in-red.html), [here](http://caroltownsendfan.blogspot.com/2007/02/whos-that-blur.html), and [here](http://caroltownsendfan.blogspot.com/2007/05/neela.html)), and she will once again be working with John Stamos.  More details to follow after the shoot.

UPDATE: Carol reports that there were several shots that might yield Carol-spotting.  In two shots, you just might see a smear of color (cream colored pants and blue denim jean jacket) in the background that is vaguely Carol-shaped: one long shot of the bleachers where Carol is across the field from her co-star John, and one where she is behind John in the stands near the third-base line.  In a third shot, outside of Wrigley Field, we may be treated to a shot of Carol's butt as we see her walk in front of John while he stands and looks at the park.  I can hardly wait!!  Set your Tivo for October 18th!

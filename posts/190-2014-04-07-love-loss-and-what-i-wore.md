# Love, Loss, and What I Wore

*Originally published 2014-04-07 on caroltownsend.com*

Greetings fans!

Spring is upon us, and it is the perfect time to see a show!  Why not come to see Carol in **Love, Loss, and What I Wore**?  Ladies, bring your men!

Performances are at 8:00pm on May 2nd, 3rd, 9th, and 10th, and 3:00pm on May the 4th (come out to celebrate [Star Wars Day](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars_Day)!)

Location is [Center Stage Theatre](http://centerstage.fairladyproductions.net/) in Naperville, [1665 Quincy Ave.](https://goo.gl/maps/Trikf),

Tickets are $20 for Adults, $15 for Students/Seniors, or $10 for children under 12 (but you probably don't want to bring your children under 12... just sayin').  For tickets and more information, [click here](http://www.evergreentheatreensemble.org/love-loss-and-what-i-wore/).

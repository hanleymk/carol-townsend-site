# Miss Ohio Wins Big in Hollywood!

*Originally published 2009-06-16 on the Blogspot site*

Greetings Fans!
As blogged [last month](http://caroltownsendfan.blogspot.com/2009/05/miss-ohio-goes-to-la.html), Miss Ohio was screened at the Dances With Films festival in Hollywood earlier this month.  I am pleased to report that Miss Ohio was awarded top honors in the Features category, the Grand Jury Award!!  You can read about it on their web site [here](http://www.danceswithfilms.com/top.html).

![2009-06-16-miss-ohio-wins-big-in-hollywood__GrandJuryAward.jpg](../images/2009-06-16-miss-ohio-wins-big-in-hollywood__GrandJuryAward.jpg)

Samantha Simon and Gregory Fitzsimmons accept the Grand Jury Award

at Dances With Films 2009.  Photo courtesy of Samantha Simon.

[Miss Ohio](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1286748/) was written and directed by Gregory Fitzsimmons, and produced by Gregory Fitzsimmons and James Francis Flynn.  Accepting the award at the Dances With Films festival were Gregory Fitzsimmons, and lead actress Samantha Simon (Carol Townsend was regrettably unavailable).

Congratulations to all who were involved in Miss Ohio!!

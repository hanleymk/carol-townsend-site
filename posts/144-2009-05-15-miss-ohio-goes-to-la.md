# Miss Ohio Goes to LA

*Originally published 2009-05-15 on the Blogspot site*

Hello Fans!

I know, it has been weeks since we had any news. After doing two plays back-to-back, Carol has been taking it easy and relaxing, and news has been slow. Aside from working for Kaplan Medical as a standardized patient, and the occasional Olay promotion, Carol has been living the life of leisure!

![2009-05-15-miss-ohio-goes-to-la__miss_ohio_cat1.jpg](../images/2009-05-15-miss-ohio-goes-to-la__miss_ohio_cat1.jpg)

Carol Townsend (left) and Samantha Simon, in Miss Ohio

That said, here is some news about one of Carol's past works. The independent film, [Miss Ohio](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1286748/), is being screened in LA at the [Dances With Films Festival](http://www.danceswithfilms.com/09_list_f_mr.html). The screening is at the Sunset 5 Theatre in West Hollywood on June 8th, at 7:15pm. Get your tickets now!

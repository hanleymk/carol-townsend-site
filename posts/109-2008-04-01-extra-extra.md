# Extra!  Extra!

*Originally published 2008-04-01 on the Blogspot site*

Greetings Fans!
You may have guessed, the previous post was a bit of fun-- although Carol would welcome the opportunity to work with [RDA](http://rdanderson.com/) on anything, should the opportunity present itself!  Happy April Fools Day!

All kidding aside, Carol will be working as an extra-- er, background actor-- tomorrow on [Unborn](http://imdb.com/title/tt1139668/), which is shooting in the Chicago area.  Unborn is a horror movie, written and directed by [David Goyer](http://imdb.com/name/nm0333060/).  This will be Carol's first venture into the horror genre.  She will once again be working with [Gary Oldman](http://imdb.com/name/nm0000198/), whom we both worked with on [The Dark Knight](http://imdb.com/title/tt0468569/) last summer.  Carol may also be working with newcomer, and brother-in-law, Charles Miceli, who has recently taken up acting after having entered semi-early-retirement.

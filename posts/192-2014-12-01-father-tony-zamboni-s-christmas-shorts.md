# Father Tony Zamboni's Christmas Shorts

*Originally published 2014-12-01 on caroltownsend.com*

Merry Christmas, fans!

Yes, I know it's only just past Thanksgiving.  But what better way to prepare for the Christmas season by seeing our favorite thespian in a Christmas-themed show?  You can do just that by visiting the James Downing Theatre this coming weekend.

What is it?
Father Tony Zamboni's Christmas Shorts... a holiday-themed short play festival.

When is it?
I just said... it's this weekend, December 5th and 6th at 7:30 PM, and 7th at 1:30 PM.

Where is it?
[The James Downing Theatre](http://jamesdowningtheatre.com/)
6740 N. Oliphant Ave.
Chicago, IL 60631

    **

Tickets are $10.  To reserve a seat, call (224) 725-3696, or click [here](http://www.brownpapertickets.com/event/969477).

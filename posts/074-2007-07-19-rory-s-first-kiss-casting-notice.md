# Rory's First Kiss Casting Notice

*Originally published 2007-07-19 on the Blogspot site*

Hello RFK/Bat Fans!
Rumor has it that the extra casting company for [Rory's First Kiss](http://imdb.com/title/tt0468569/) has changed.  The new casting company can be found at [http://rfkcasting.com](http://rfkcasting.com/).  They are accepting applications for extras, either by mail or electronically (details and application form are on the site).
Good luck!

7/19 UPDATE: They are apparently not accepting online submissions (and have removed the text from the web site that said that they were).  Snail Mail submissions only.

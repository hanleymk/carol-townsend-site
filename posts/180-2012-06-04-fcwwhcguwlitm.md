# FCWWHCGUWLITM

*Originally published 2012-06-04 on the Blogspot site*

It's starting off to be a busy summer with Carol appearing [in the graveyard](http://caroltownsendfan.blogspot.com/2012/05/when-was-war-of-1812.html) on June 30th, and now in another upcoming show starting June 15th.  [Harvest Productions](http://harveststagenet.ipage.com/index.html) will be presenting **For Christian Women Who Have Considered Giving Up When Life Is Too Much**.  Yes, I believe this will go into the record books as the longest titled show Carol has appeared in so far!

The show follows seven Christian women who lose their way when their lives are interrupted by tragedy, and their struggle to reconnect with the God who loves them.  Carol will play... one of the seven women!

June 15th - July 8th *

Fridays 7:30pm

Saturdays 2:00pm and 7:30pm

Sundays 2:00pm

That's a total of 16 shows-- Surely you can find time to come see one of them!

 * (Carol will not be appearing in the show on June 30th as she will be in Oakwood Cemetery, so that's 15 shows with Carol, and 1 without, for those keeping track.)

[Stage 773 Theater](http://www.stage773.com/)

1225 W. Belmont Ave.

Chicago, IL
